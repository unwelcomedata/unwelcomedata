# @unwelcomedata

**Data on things people have opinions about.**

I take public data on questions people argue about, work it end to end, and publish
the whole thing — the charts, the underlying dataset, and where every number came
from. Sources cited, methodology shown, conclusions left to you.

### → Start here: **[unwelcomedata.github.io](https://unwelcomedata.github.io/)**

That's the front door. Everything lives there. The projects below each have their
own page with charts, the data, and full sourcing.

## Projects

- **[Where Americans are moving](https://unwelcomedata.github.io/state-migration/)** — state-to-state migration and the income that moves with it. IRS + Census.
- **[DUI across the states](https://unwelcomedata.github.io/dui-by-state/)** — enforcement, penalties, and outcomes compared across all 51 states. FARS + official sources.
- **[Abortion vs. leading causes of death](https://unwelcomedata.github.io/abortion-cause-of-death/)** — how abortion compares to other causes of death, by group. CDC WONDER + Guttmacher.
- **[Highest-grossing films](https://unwelcomedata.github.io/highest-grossing-films/)** — "highest-grossing" depends on how you count. Box office through three lenses.
- **[Taskmaster, normalized](https://unwelcomedata.github.io/taskmaster/)** — contestant performance across 21 series, scaled so short and long series compare fairly.

## How it works

- Every source is public and cited.
- Every project shows its method, not just its conclusion.
- The data is downloadable. Check the work.
